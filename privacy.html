Privacy Policy

From M.E.S.S. to Map

Development and Testing

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

From M.E.S.S. to Map is a content organization service currently in research, development, and testing. This policy describes our current website and our planned TikTok integration.

2. Current Development Status

The application is not currently available as a public service. Public account connections, registrations, and paid services are not available.

We are evaluating technical capabilities and authorized content management workflows in a controlled testing environment.

3. Website Information

Our current website is informational. It does not request TikTok login credentials or collect TikTok account information through a public account connection.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with visits under its own privacy practices.

4. Planned TikTok Integration

We are developing optional integrations through TikTok's authorized API services.

With a creator's permission, the application may access basic profile information and available public video metadata, including video identifiers, descriptions, publication dates, and engagement statistics.

We will not ask creators to provide their TikTok passwords.

5. Intended Use of Data

Authorized information is intended to support searchable content inventories, record reconciliation, identification of missing or duplicate content records, and creator-directed organization.

Additional features may be developed and tested as the project evolves.

6. Data Storage and Security

The systems for processing and storing authorized creator data are still being developed.

Before enabling account connections, this policy will be updated to describe actual storage practices, service providers, safeguards, retention, and sharing arrangements.

7. User Authorization and Deletion

Future account integrations will require authorization from the account owner through TikTok.

Users may revoke applicable permissions through TikTok. Procedures for requesting deletion of data held by From M.E.S.S. to Map will be established before account connections become available.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may change as the application is developed. Updates will be published here with a revised effective date.
