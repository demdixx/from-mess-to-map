What We're Building

From M.E.S.S. to Map is a developing content organization service designed to help creators inventory, organize, and rediscover their existing digital content.

Our Approach

We believe technology should support a creator's unique voice, not replace it. Our goal is to reduce repetitive work while helping creators better understand and use their own content.

Development Status
